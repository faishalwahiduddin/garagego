Car and Bike Service History: Tidy Year After Year

By Faishal Wahiduddin · 2026-10-04 · 5 min read

GarageGo logs your car and bike service history with date, mileage, workshop, and cost. Every service is stored per vehicle so history stays tidy year after year. Register vehicles, log each service, and review monthly cost recaps.

Contents
  1. Register your cars and bikes
  2. Log every service fully
  3. Review vehicle cost recaps

Register your cars and bikes#

Every service is logged with date, mileage, workshop, and cost. Per-vehicle data stays neatly separate so the family car and daily bike never mix.

Date, mileage, and cost#

Date marks when service happened, mileage marks distance at the time, cost logs spending. Three columns answer when the last service was at a glance.

Log every service fully#

Manage many vehicles in one list: each family member's cars and bikes. Pick the vehicle before logging so each history stays intact.

Review vehicle cost recaps#

Monthly and yearly vehicle-cost recaps summarize all spending. Service, oil, and fuel spending appear separately so cost sources read clearly.

  • Register your cars and bikes with their STNK tax dates.
  • Log each service with date and mileage.
  • Write workshop and cost per job.
  • Keep history separate per vehicle.
  • Review cost recaps monthly and yearly.

Monthly and yearly recaps#

Compare recaps across months to see cost trends. Recaps help decide when a vehicle needs extra attention before breaking down.

FAQ

What data is logged per service?

Date, mileage, workshop, and cost of each service job.

Can I manage many vehicles?

Yes. Manage cars and bikes in one list with separate histories.

Is the data safe?

Safe. All data stays on-device and is never sent to a server.

Are there cost recaps?

Yes. Vehicle-cost recaps per month and per year.

Does it fit couriers?

Yes. Riders and couriers living off vehicles benefit from history and reminders.
