Oil-Change Reminders: By Distance or Time

By Faishal · 2026-10-04 · 4 min read

GarageGo oil reminders follow mileage or time intervals, keeping car and bike engines guarded without memorizing. Log each oil change with its mileage, set the next target, and get reminded when it is due.

Contents
  1. Log each oil change
  2. Set distance or time targets
  3. Get reminded on time

Log each oil change

Log each oil change with date and mileage. Per-vehicle oil history reveals usage patterns: routine long trips or short hops only.

Mileage as the main reference

Mileage is the main reference for the next oil schedule. Distance targets count from the last oil-change mileage, not from memory. [STAT-NEEDED: average user oil-change interval] [CITE-NEEDED: vehicle maker recommendations]

Set distance or time targets

Set targets by mileage or time interval per recommendations. Two modes cover different styles: far daily use and occasional near use.

Get reminded on time

Reminders fire when distance or time targets are reached. No memorizing needed; the app flags which vehicle is due.

  • Log each oil change with its mileage.
  • Set distance or time-interval targets.
  • Pick distance, time, or both modes.
  • Get reminded when targets are reached.
  • Log again after the next oil change.

Never miss a schedule

Promptly log the next oil change after it happens so targets always count from fresh data. This small habit keeps schedules accurate. [STAT-NEEDED: vehicles late on oil changes] [CITE-NEEDED: internal lateness data]

FAQ

Which reference is used?

Mileage since the last change, or time interval, or both.

Is it per vehicle?

Yes. Oil targets and history stay separate per vehicle.

When do reminders fire?

When the set distance or time target is reached.

Is the data safe?

Safe. All data stays on-device.

Does it fit daily bikes?

Yes. Daily bikes, family cars, and courier vehicles are supported.
