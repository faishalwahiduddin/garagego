Yearly STNK Tax: Never Miss the Deadline

By Faishal Wahiduddin · 2026-10-04 · 4 min read

GarageGo reminds you of yearly and five-yearly STNK tax deadlines per vehicle. Register each vehicle's tax date, get reminded before expiry, and pay on time. No more fines from forgotten dates.

Register each vehicle's tax date#

Each vehicle's STNK tax date is registered once and stored permanently. Date data grounds all later reminders with no re-entry.

Yearly and five-yearly#

Yearly tax renews each year; five-yearly covers plate replacement. Both are tracked separately so they never mix.

Get reminded before expiry#

Reminders arrive before expiry with enough lead to prepare funds and documents. Lead time leaves room to process without rushing.

Pay on time every year#

Paying on time yearly keeps vehicles legal to ride. Payment history is stored as proof of orderly administration.

  • Separate yearly and five-yearly schedules.
  • Get reminded before expiry.
  • Pay on time every year.
  • Update the next tax date.

Avoid late fines#

After paying, update the next tax date so the reminder cycle continues. One minute of updating saves late fines.
